Overview

Garuda is a robot created through the collaboration of Aruna’s parents and Professor Astinayudha.  Her parents have foreseen the declining peace between nations and KARMA’s schemes underneath it all. Seeing a potential catastrophe sooner or later, Garuda was created to protect Aruna along with the cryosleep pod. Astinayudha proposed the idea in the emergency that Aruna’s body could not stabilize in time, and he’s forced to evacuate and leave the lab. When he eventually left, Garuda was activated as part of the defensive protocols set in place.

**In Act One**

As Aruna reawakens, she is struggling to fit with her body and the post-war state of Bali. Armed with integrated communication system, battle protocols, and smart AI analyzer, Garuda sets his mission to protect and guide Aruna.

**Goal:** Guide, protect, and be a companion for Aruna

**Obstacle:** Bali proves to be a hostile place for them both

**Arc:** Garuda becomes the mentor and parental figure for Aruna after she reawakened

**In Act Two**

Garuda states its suspicion towards the bandit faction after their meeting, instructing Aruna to keep her distance and stay vigilant. However, his cautions fell on her deaf ears. Aruna’s growing boldness also presents a new risk variable in her survival. They eventually have a fallout, and he seeks to reforge their relations while trying to keep her out of danger. And he kept that unwavering loyalty even after disaster and betrayal struck them.

**Goal:** Keep Aruna safe from herself and from the bandits

**Obstacle:** Aruna grows more and more rebellious

**Arc:** Garuda persists on warning Aruna to be vigilant and not dive into danger or trusting the suspicious Bandit Leader so easily. Aruna continues to disregard his warnings until they got into trouble which costed Garuda’s life

**In Act Three**

Garuda’s remains were brought by Aruna to a temple that took her in after she escaped the deadly encounter between the Bandit Leader and KARMA. It was then reassembled into a headpiece for Aruna. A symbol of her redemption and newfound will.

Key Relationships

·         Aruna – The sole purpose of his being, the reason why he is created. Though he does not possess what we consider as human emotion, his loyalty mirrors that of a loving guardian towards Aruna. He is protective and patient when it comes to Aruna’s safety and growth.

·         Astinayudha – Garuda’s analytics could not match the bandit leader’s identity to his memory cache. Yet, it resulted in a big enough percentage to also not be noteworthy. Hence, why he maintained vigilance and a certain level of trust towards him. Argumenting that someone who’s potentially close to them not revealing their identity right away is suspicious and might hide ulterior motives.
