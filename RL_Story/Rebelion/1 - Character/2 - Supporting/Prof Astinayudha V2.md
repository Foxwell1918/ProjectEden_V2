## Overview
Astinayudha is a renowed bio-technical researcher and surgeon. He now holds the last bastion of human resistance in Bali against Karma's opression.

He leads the [[Banaspati Bandits]] who is a guerilla fighter adamant on liberating Bali whatever is the cost.

He orchestrated raids and assaults on Karma's assets and transport routes to weaken her power.

He quickly recognises that [[Aksara]] is the girl he operated on, the result of his and her parents combined efforts and intellegence to save the girl's life and ensure the success of the first [[Quantum Core]] powered machine.

He was compassionate then, but now changed by the post-apocalypric world and seeks to find extreme measures to create change. Including taking [[Aksara]]'s [[Quantum Core]] if needs to, and depriving her of the life he once breathes onto her.

## Appearance
Astinayudha is covered with mechanical facade. THick goggles to combat the hat of the sun, supported with advanced features to help him in combat or information gathering.

A filter and voice modulation to protect his breathing from dangerous substances, and voice modulation to hide his identity.

He wore a utility jacket that doubles as a body armour, it holds different armaments, explosives and tools to ensure heis always reardy for combat.

He is tall for a balinese.

He don't have any cybernetics, but rather he uses augmentation to help him traverse the dangerous land.
