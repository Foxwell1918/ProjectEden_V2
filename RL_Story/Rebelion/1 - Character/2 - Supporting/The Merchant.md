
# Overview

The Merchant is a peculiar and mysterious figure that emerged in [[Sukawati E]] after Aruna’s fight with [[Rangda]] in the ruins of [Outlander 2](https://www.notion.so/Chapter-2-Flow-afe29137665944aaa0b97980ad234bd8?pvs=21) and the Troopers in Sukawati. He wears a goggle to protect his eyes from debris and scanners. He sometimes hide his wide, welcoming smile with a scarf around his neck for similar reasons. The merchant has a very open-arms, positive personality that attracts conversations. Perhaps, a trait that he developed for his trade. Be it goods or valuable information. He will be the one that sends Aruna on a quest to gather parts to get the solar panel of Sukawati running again.

He is also proficient in electronics, making his living from fixing and making various electronics and tools. A skill and knowledge inherited from his younger age. Some even say he was actually a pretty well known engineer before KARMA came to Bali. Perhaps that is also how he has connections to the [[Banaspati Bandits]]’ leader. Working anonymously to provide information through encrypted messages.

Sells informations directly to [[Prof Astinayudha V1]]

### Physical Appearance

The Merchant is **silver-haired** which reflects his age which is around **late 40s and early 50s.** In general, his choice of clothing is **similar to other Sukawati civilians** and merchants. Since he repairs and scavenges a lot of electrical related objects, he **keeps a set of tools with him** for ease of use. One of his discernible feature is his **implanted Bionic Eye** on one of his eye that he made himself. It helps with his vision and to scan and analyse his surroundings.
