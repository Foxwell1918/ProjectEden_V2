## Background
### Early Life
Jaggernaut was made as a first line of Crowd Control Trooper Prototypes, he was designed big and scary for the purpose of giving a more scary and commanding feeling to the people he commands, mostly for the civilians and rebels.

He was sent to Sukawati to deal with the civil disobedience there. the Artists Protests, 

Back when his task was only policing, he downloaded data regarding humanities, arts, philosophy in order to have a dialogue with the protesters, to speak their language, garner support, advertising himself as the "Gentle Giant" He eventually gained power around Sukawati area.

But behind the scenes, Karma keeps gaining power, with more power she usurped, the weaker the justice systems, the weaker the state.

There is another protest, now with no Law stopping them, he silenced the protest and built a forward operating base to control the town. Year after year nails that sticks out were hammered down, and after several years, the boards are tidy.

With the subjugation of Sukawati, he was promoted by Karma into being a Commander and Sector Supervisor, giving him over the fortress that he built. And since then, he sat on the fortress, overlooking the GWK area, expanding Karma's reach.

His first deployment into the Southern Fortress to construct an outpost for [[Operation Ravana WIP]]

Since then, resource within his fortress thinned due to the survey, Troopers are sent south, electricity were needed to charge companies of Troopers that is going to be sent.
