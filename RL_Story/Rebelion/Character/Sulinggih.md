# Overview

Ida Bagus Wayan Yogi has been the head priest (Sulinggih) of the Renon Temple Complex for as long as the locals can remember. **Ministering the areas around Renon including Sukawati**. **The locals look up to him as a spiritual leader and a respected wise elder that oversees the spiritual and social needs of the common folk.** He talks rather **slowly with a soothing voice** where one would be eager to listen to his advices. The Sulinggih also holds some intrinsic power when it comes to the control of this area of Bali. **In the past, he had secured a treaty with KARMA to make Pura Renon as a safe-haven that can not be attacked in exchange of bringing South Bali into KARMA’s fold.** An initiative that saved thousands of lives but an action he also contemplates and **regrets** until this moment.

Sulinggih regrets letting Karma take south of bali in return of the safety of Pura Renon.

In chapter 2, he welcomes Aruna in Pura Renon and gives her a place to rest awhile and gather her thoughts. 

**He offers respite to Aruna that has been struggling to find her purpose amidst the chaos and battles she had recently fought.** He also tells Aruna about **the prophecy of Bali’s liberation**. **A folk tale about a figure that will save Bali from evil**.

In chapter 5, he and the Renon Temple once again becomes a safe harbour for Aruna after suffering defeat and the loss of Garuda. Through his advice, Aruna embraces her destiny and purpose to be the prophesized saviour of Bali.

### Physical Appearance

Sulinggih Wayan Yogi is **very old.** His face is **heavily wrinkled** with **dark circles underneath his eyes.** All of his **hair has turned white and curled**. He has a **full, long beard** that reaches his chest. Like traditional priests of old, he dons __a white robe and a ritual hat (Udeng_)._* He keeps everything in the temple **clean and tidy** which also shows in his attire too.
