Ekaterina Sergenova or better known as KARMA, conceals her aging body with [[transhuman modifications]], replacing and enhancing parts of her biological body with fine carbon and steel. Her refined appearance glittered dazzling accessories and lavish-looking coat conjure an image of peak human advancement and order. The pristine lavishness of her appearance is irony to those who oppose her and inspiration to those who look up to her.

Overview

KARMA’s rise to power begun as a paragon of AI research and development from [[Russia’s leading space program]]. Her knack of finding the most pragmatic and efficient solutions to intricate problems go terrifyingly together resulting in her technology’s effectiveness. It is almost the lack of conscience and norm that led to her ingenuity some of her peers said. Driven with the unstoppable desire to mold the future of humanity with her own two hands, she developed a worldwide network of AI to cut inefficiencies within humanity’s most vital energy sources, from the sun to nuclear powerplants. It is with this technological breakthrough that she won the hearts of many [[nation leaders in 1970]]. As time goes by, KARMA slowly became the ruling hand of the world with nations and humanity itself heavily relying on her technology. After the awakening of [[Aruna]] in 2110 that proves the existence of the [[Quantum Core]], a self-sustaining renewable energy source, KARMA has diverted her attention to eliminating the only threat to her monopoly.

**In Act One**

KARMA through her drones and army of troopers detected an emergence of a highly dense [[energy source]] in Bali. It is in the form of a cyborg young woman with the fighting capabilities and lethal technology. After realizing that this must be the Quantum Core that has been the source of Aruna’s power, KARMA makes it her priority to eliminate Aruna.

**Goal**: Learn more about the mysterious reawakening of Aruna

**Obstacle:** Aruna proves to be a capable force that can’t be exterminated easily

**Arc:** Underestimating Aruna, KARMA fails to immediately eliminate the threat of her capitalist empire

**In Act Two**

[[The bandit rebellion]] has been nothing but a small nuisance to KARMA’s control in Bali. But now, their leader has convinced Aruna to join their side. Perhaps it is time for KARMA to take out both at once. Or even use them to destroy each other.

**Goal:** Find out a way to take out Aruna who is now allying with the bandit rebellion.

**Obstacle:** Their combined strength becomes noticeably challenging for KARMA’s forces to handle

**Arc:** KARMA has deemed the [[bandits]] of Bali as negligible until they joined forces with Aruna. Their growing ambition proves to be a blessing in disguise as KARMA found a way to weaken both the rebels and Aruna.

**In Act Three**

After successfully eliminating [[Garuda]], KARMA moves in to deliver the killing blow, preferably by herself. After taunting Aruna, she arrives in Bali and fortifies her base in anticipation for Aruna’s attempt to avenge her fallen companion. Ultimately, she failed and Aruna successfully conquered KARMA’s base in Bali.

**Goal:** Eliminate the Quantum Core for good

**Obstacle:** Aruna launched a lethal assault to her base in Bali

**Arc:** KARMA fails to eliminate Aruna and take care of the Quantum Core. Now, Aruna’s victory sends echoes of liberation and change throughout Bali and possibly the world.

Key Relationships

·         Aruna – The sole threat of KARMA’s total control of humanity. Aruna’s Quantum Core provides a self-sustaining powerful energy source that if studied properly, can render KARMA’s technological monopoly obsolete.

·         Aruna’s Parents – The only researchers who almost put an early stop to Ekaterina’s ambitions to rule and monopolize the world. Their research in Quantum Core had been the bane of her technological empire and proved to be the thorn on her side.

Need: Eliminate all competition (Aruna’s Quantum Core technology)
