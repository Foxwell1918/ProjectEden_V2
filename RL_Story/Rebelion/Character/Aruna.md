**Physical Appearance:**

Due to the cryo-sleep state she was induced into; her metabolism slows down to a significant amount, leaving her biological age to only be progressing by around 3 years.  Aruna appears to be a 19-year-old female whose body is made of an advanced biological frame powered by a self-sustaining energy source named the [[Quantum Core]]. The only original human body that remains appears to be only her head. Imbued with advanced technology using [[transhuman modifications]], the cybernetical body itself is filled with intricacies and advanced mechanical functions.

**Tropes to Avoid:**

-          Overly showy/gaudy with newfound powers

We want Aruna to grow in confidence but in a more natural way. Her initial reactions to her new powers and body are uncertainty rather than over excitement. Direct reference to what we don't want: Forspoken MC.

-          Annoying teenager trope

Aruna’s character arc is self-discovery and along that comes a time when Aruna feels overconfident about her newfound powers. We want her to be cantankerous and trigger-happy but not brash and nosy.

Overview

Aruna was the daughter of two world renowned scientists working on the advancement of alternative energy. 

After failing to win the favor of the world leaders during the [[San Fransico conferences]], Aruna was sent to [[Bali]] to by her parents to shelter her from rising unrests. 

Since then, Aruna spent the rest of her youth living with her grandmother. 

Until a fateful accident resulted in the loss of her grandmother and severe damage to Aruna’s legs and internal organs. 

To preserve her life and prepare her for the future, her parents worked with [[Astinayudha]] to give Aruna a cybernetical body and implant the Quantum Core within it. 

She was then induced to a cryosleep to avoid complications. Now, she travels with her trusted guardian to bring change to this apocalyptic future.

**In Act One**

As Aruna begins to adjust with her new body, she is struggling to fit in this hostile new future that is very different from what she remembered from her past. She must learn how to survive and thrive in this world.

**Goal:** Survive and learn more about this changed future

**Obstacle:** The current world is hostile towards her

**Arc:** Aruna awakened in a body and world that feels very foreign to her. It is no longer her original body nor the reality that she can remember. She must learn to navigate through this apocalyptic waste that is Bali in 2110.

**In Act Two**

With her growth in strength and confidence, Aruna uncooperatively charges headlong into any challenges the apocalyptic future Bali can offer after learning more about KARMA and her **global** influence. Here, she meets a peculiarly familiar figure who lures Aruna with acceptance and purpose.

**Goal:** Work with [[Astinayudha]] (Bandit Leader) to build strength to rebel against KARMA.

**Obstacle:** The Bandit Leader schemes to take advantage of Aruna and [[Garuda]].

**Arc:** Aruna finds a glimmer of hope and a sense of kinship within the [[Banaspati Bandits]]. She now feels powerful and neglects Garuda’s advice to not trust so easily or too rash in combat to help the bandits rebel against KARMA. Until it was all too late.

**In Act Three**

The betrayal, the loss of Garuda and the defeat Aruna suffered has brought her low. After retreating and finding new allies and a new perspective, Aruna swore to be the agent of change for the betterment of humanity. Confronting her new reality for good as well as KARMA’s iron tight hold in Bali.

**Goal:** Confront KARMA and liberate Bali from her influence

**Obstacle:** Aruna needs to strike at KARMA’s Bali HQ alone without Garuda

**Arc:** After her loss and retreat to [[Pura Renon]], Aruna found her true purpose by embracing herself and this world’s reality. She now storms KARMA’s center of operations in Bali to restore harmony in Bali.

Key Relationships

·         Garuda - Garuda is an intelligent robot that was created to be Aruna’s guide and companion in the chance that Aruna wakes up far in the future. Garuda is calm and calculated in every situation while showing signs that it cares most about Aruna’s safety and wellbeing. It is the last semblance of a parental figure/mentor for Aruna.

·         Professor/Bandit Leader – He was responsible for Aruna’s cybernetic surgery and cryosleep. Once a hopeful and kind young man, the world had forged him into a pragmatic and calculating figure who is willing to sacrifice lives to ensure humanity’s cause. Including Aruna’s own life.

·         KARMA – She is the very threat of Aruna’s existence. KARMA seeks nothing but the elimination or seizure of the Quantum Core that is the source of Aruna’s life. She cares not for Aruna herself, rather seeing her Quantum Core as a direct competitor in her capitalist monopoly.

Need: Realize that to be truly free, one must be ready to face one’s own self and the world (Acceptance)

Flaw: Naive (Believing that with an almost full cyborg body with a powerful energy source, she can blend in with the oppressed natives)
