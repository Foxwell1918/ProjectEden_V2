**Physical Appearance:**

Just like the other Banaspati Bandits, Astinayudha’s countenance is a covered with a mechanical facade. Thick goggles to combat the heat of the sun and supported with advanced features to help him in combat or information gathering. A filtering and voice altering mask protects his nose from inhaling poisonous fumes, airborne toxins, or burnt deadly elements that can pose risk to his respiratory system. He wears a utility jacket that doubles as his body armor. It holds different armaments, explosives, and tools to ensure he is always ready for combat and subterfuge. He is quite tall for a Balinese with quite fit for his age. He has not replaced any of his body parts with cybernetics, but he does have augmentations to help with his quality of life or combat. On his back, a large sigil of the Banaspati Bandits decorates his jacket, like many other of his bandit crew.

**Overview**

Formerly a renowned bio-technical researcher and surgeon, Astinayudha now holds the last bastion of human resistance in Bali against [[Karma]]’s oppressive control. He leads the [[Banaspati Bandits]], a group of rebels who are adamant on liberating Bali whatever heavy the cost. He is the one who orchestrated raids and guerilla assaults on KARMA’s assets and transport routes to weaken her. Astinayudha quickly recognized Aruna was the same girl he helped and operated on. The result of his and her parents’ combined efforts and intelligence to save the girl’s life and ensure the success of the first Quantum Core-powered machine. He who was compassionate now changed by this apocalyptic world and seeks to find extreme measures to create change. Including taking Aruna’s Quantum Core if he needs to and depriving her of the life he once worked tirelessly to save.

**In Act One**

Artinayudha has a more passive role during act one as he does not have the means to know about the reawakening. However, his scouts have reported of whispers among the common people that a mysterious cyborg has suddenly appeared. And he might have a hunch about who this cyborg is. His intuition proved correct as the female cyborg turned out to be the same girl he helped 30 years ago.

**Goal:** Learn more about the mysterious reawakening of Aruna

**Obstacle:** Have no resource to investigate further

**Arc:** Astinayudha who has been forged and burned by the fires of war now finds a new hope. A chance to liberate Bali: The Quantum-Core.

**In Act Two**

Astinayudha welcomed Aruna’s arrival with open arms. With her help, he plans to improve and upgrade the Banaspati Bandit base. Adding her into their ranks also bolstered their effectiveness in combat. However, he noticed that Aruna’s brashness proves to be an inconsistent variable. The [[Quantum Core]] will constantly be at risk with Aruna’s aggressiveness in combat. Perhaps, it is better if it stays with him than her. Moreover, her Garuda companion has rare high-quality parts that can benefit the bandits.

**Goal:** Acquire the Quantum Core for himself

**Obstacle:** Aruna objects as she does not want to give it up

**Arc:** Astinayudha betrays Aruna’s trust as he plans to take the Quantum Core and scrap Garuda for parts.

**In Act Three**

The Bandit Leader of Banaspati conspires to betray Aruna and Garuda. The Quantum Core is better suited in his hands to strengthen the rebellion rather than a life support for Aruna. Aruna is one individual after all, immature and mortal. But as a powersource for the bandit base? The Quantum Core can help him field a proper army. After failing to secure the Quantum Core for himself and beaten by Aruna and Garuda, Astinayudha’s life ends in the hands of KARMA. She has set a trap for both Aruna and Astinayudha to end any semblance of rebellion in Bali.

**Goal:** Acquire the Quantum Core for himself

**Obstacle:** Fight against Aruna and Garuda and perhaps KARMA at the same time

**Arc:** Astinayudha fails and loses his life in the attempt to take the Quantum Core from Aruna.

Key Relationships

·         Aruna – The very same girl who was crippled and slowly dying away, the very same girl he operated on and worked so hard on saving, suddenly appeared in this apocalyptic future. His hope of her recovery had dissipated years ago, but now he is glad to see her alive. His hard work had indeed paid off. But now, he thinks that there is a better use for the Quantum Core than Aruna’s life support. A strong energy source like that can strengthen his opposition against KARMA.

·         KARMA – He burns with rage whenever he sees KARMA’s countenance or name. He considers KARMA to be a blight to humanity itself that has reached his homeland, disrupting the harmony and precious natural wonders of Bali. He strives for eradicating her presence in Bali and even the world, the sole drive and purpose of his life.
