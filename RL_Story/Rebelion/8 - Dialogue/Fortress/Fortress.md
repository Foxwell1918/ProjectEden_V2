	1. The Materials are taken from the Loading Docks
	2. The materials will be then inputted to the 3D Printers
	3. When the 3D Printing process finish, the finished parts then got assembled and moved to a hanging conveyor belt
2. Hanging Conveyor belt.
	1. The Unactivated TROOPERS are put in conveyor belt up on 2nd Level
	2. a CORE is inserted to the Unactivated Troopers
	3. After insertion, the Troopers then become ACTIVE
3. Quality Assurance
	1. The TROOPERS then undergoes a QC process on the 4th floor
		1. If accepted then goes down gently to the loading docs
		2. If rejected, thrown into the reject pile
