
Sukawati Traditional Market was already a hotspot of local commerce far before KARMA arrived in Bali. The remnants of this very active commercial site can still be seen in its towering structures covered with neon signs and banners. Monitors displaying eye-catching designs and colours draw any potential spenders in. The friendly greetings from the merchants offering their products add to the sense of community and belonging even for weary travellers.

Although, now, the market is but a shell of its former self. It has been months since KARMA cut off the market’s power supply to repress growing tensions within the civilians. Ever since Sukawati was brought into KARMA’s fold and presence in Bali, the merchants of Sukawati has been a constant glimmering ember of rebellion. Hence, when an offense took place, KARMA was quick to punish Sukawati by stripping electricity off from them.
