## Overview
KARMA through her drones and army of troopers detected an emergence of a highly dense [[energy source E]] in Bali. It is in the form of a cyborg young woman with the fighting capabilities and lethal technology. After realizing that this must be the Quantum Core that has been the source of Aruna’s power, KARMA makes it her priority to eliminate Aruna.
