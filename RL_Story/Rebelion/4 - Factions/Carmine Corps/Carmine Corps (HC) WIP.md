## Overview
Carmine Corps is the first Corporation that can harness the power of Artificial Intelligence and put them into robots, they created AI systems, powerful military robots that can adapt quickly in the battlefield using AI technology.

Carmine Corps uses this Military Contract to produce robots to seize power within the Russian Government, eventually growing in so much power, they have their own power.
