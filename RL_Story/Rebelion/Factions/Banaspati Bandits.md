### Overview

The Banaspati Bandits are the Balinese resistance group that engages in guerrilla warfare with KARMA’s ever-growing presence in the isle. They started as splintered regional groups with little to no organization. It was until someone brought the scattered resistance under one banner and proclaimed themselves Banaspati. The name derives from malevolent spirits believed to be protectors of the land- Mountains and Forests, from evil-doers and those who do not belong. The name mirrors their hatred towards KARMA’s presence in their native land and their tactics that involve in arson and subterfuge.

### History

In 2093, after the early stages of KARMA’s campaign of global monopoly, the first ever semblance of a resistance group formed in Denpasar. Starting peaceful demonstrations to aggressive boycotts and rhetoric campaigns to fight off KARMA’s influence from encroaching Indonesia and Bali.

In 2095, the first **armed** resistance groups are formed all over Bali, starting a guerrilla war over the following years, attacking transport caravans and military bases, such as Kintamani and Denpasar.

In July 2098, an organized and heavily armed guerrilla force intercepted a major KARMA military mobilization to the north of Bali, aiming for the occupation of Buleleng and Banjar. Their success is attributed to the leadership of a mysterious figure who dons a Barong mask. This victory rallied a lot of the resistance group under his banner. In August 2098, alongside Indonesia’s Independence day, the group carried their momentous success and announced the establishment of Banaspati. A resistance group aimed solely to drive KARMA out of Bali.

By early 2101, they have established a Bali-wide underground network of resistance, occupying strategic grounds as their base of operations. Ever since then, they continued their fight in small scale battles and skirmishes in an attempt to slow down KARMA’s rapid growth.

In 2102, through KARMA’s constant propaganda and rapid developments, the resistance group has been named synonymously with Bandits. The name Banaspati Bandits has stuck ever since. They themselves accepted the moniker, seeing it as a Robin Hood-like imagery in their fight for liberation.

### Technology

The Banaspati are far from primitive. Especially under their current leader, they are very well known to scavenge, reuse, and repurpose KARMA’s technology as their own. It is uncommon for the fighters of the group to be enhanced with the very same materials, weapons, and technology one might find in KARMA’s troopers. Their weaponry is often modified and custom made. Very rarely do they used mass-produced weaponry as they do not have the facilities to do so.

Another byproduct of their customized arsenal of weaponry are a plethora of explosives and incendiaries. This is in line with their way of fighting and missions that requires them to quickly sabotage and destroy key objectives.

### Battle Tactics

There are very few cases where they fight head-on or as a vanguard in bigger battles. Banaspati usually remains hidden underground, in forests, or their own bases where they waited for a convoy or a transport caravan to ambush. Thus, preferring their battles to be quick and decisive which led to their close quarters focus on their combat strategies. For bigger targets such as raids and subterfuge on key assets, they will plan out routes for entry and escapes. Their coordination also comes to display as there will be smaller detachments with clear objectives and functions. A successful Banaspati assault and raid usually ends up in the asset turning into a bright sea of flames.
