Dwarves lives in the caverns and dug deep

They are cavemen. and in this pre-historic timeline, they dwell in caves and does expeditions every hunt seasons and return


1.Regarding Professor Welton.

a. Species and Age

Professor Welton is a human male, in his 60s, although he has slight elven ancestry but this is so little that it doesn't concern us

b. His core thesis

Professor Welton's core thesis is to break the notion that the Elves are the colonizing, conquering race. until his thesis the world history is told by the old Men viewpoint since when it is began to be written, there are no Elves left, and so it is written from the records of the Northern Empire, which is biased.

c. His Reputation

Professor Welton is known in University of Edenfield because he was a student there himself, carrying out all sorts of mischiefs that some are already becoming tradition in the University itself. He was also one of the officers who defended in the Siege of Edenfield in the Edenfield Civil War. although he personally doesn't want to be remembered as that, most people respected Welton for his deeds in the civil war.


2.Regarding the State of The World

  

a. The Present Day.
Professor Welton wrote when the Elves and Dwarves are finally extinct or are now already merging with the human population that their original ancestry has been merged. Professor Welton has been mentored by one of the Last Elves called Catherine. who has taught in University of Edenfield but chose a nomadic lifestyle by travelling the continents teaching her arts of Alchemy. which as Professor Welton learnt, she had done for 3000 years. Catherine taught her arts of Alchemy under the name Casnius Bolos, her own mentor, thus making her contribution to the arts of Alchemy relatively unknown. Welton is really close to Catherine when she was alive, she was his own mentor, close friend and distant aunt, so the way he wrote her might be a bit personal. but Catherine herself is an eccentric figure, so although she had passed he might still throw some jabs here and there in humor and respect of her. in the Present World, magic bagan to diminish, fewer users are around, and University of Edenfield began to get less and less applicant, preferring to enter the new Edenfield Intitute of Technology, which Welton had rivalries about.

b. The Accepted History
So far in the continents, what most people believe about the Dwarves and Elves are romanticed or brutalised version of the true history. one part of the continent believes that Elves are murderous, colonising and brutalising race, whilst the other believes that they are the Chosen People of the lord, the angel itself and had become a figure of worship. Welton wanted to argue that the Dwarves and The Elves are creatures just like any other, and they have history of evolution that it is detailed in Catherine's personal library.

c. The Nature of Magic.
Magic is understood by each disciplines differently. for Welton as an Alchemist, he believes that magic is nothing but a tool for him to shape the things he wanted to shape, but to some others it can mean differently. The branch of magic Welton is using are what categorised as Academic Magic. Magic that almost anyone can learn given the practice and study. but there are other different magics. there are Clerical Magic that can only be learnt in the Cathedrals of Orders under deep meditations and openness to their belief, some form of Dark Magic can only be learnt under apprenticeship from a master who had mastered the crafts, and cannot be learnt on text. Magic in it on itself has no morality of Good and Evil, Magic is a tool, it is the Magician who have to chose between its good nor evil.

3.Regarding Professor Welton's alchemical Approach to History.

a. Key Scientific Discoveries
Professor Welton mostly uses his Alchemical Knowledge to sample artefact from old Dwarven and Elven ruins, to try getting an exact date on the ruin's true heritage, from old food remains from jugs and vases to try re-making their old food, trying to figure out what is their agriculture is like, even working with the Merfolks to trace their lineage back to their Elven heritage by which he had found that the Merfolks are the closest race who is a direct and pure relation to the Proto-Elven race, whom capital city had sunk and had adapted to live under water.

b. His Tools.
Professor Welton works closely with Historians, fellow Alchemists and Automaton Engineers to make his tools, he is mostly use it to trace back how long a material has been made, and how old it is. whilst sometime examining key magical items like amunets, staves, and crowns to try to reverse-engineer their potentcy as a magical conduits. although those reverse-engineering projects are usually taken over by the Conjurers, he is still a key member of the research for its history
