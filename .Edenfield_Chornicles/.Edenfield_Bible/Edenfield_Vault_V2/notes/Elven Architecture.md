Elven Architecture are often placed quite high up with a basement/storage/servant headquarters at the very bottom, due to their origin of living high up at the tree, this translates to their architecture

In the Pre-Historic era, they live high up the White Trees, and are off the ground which is their safety from the predators in the Illusory Forest

This however become a problem during the Great Floods, which flooded their grounds, making them unable to go to ground again

This made then develop skills of swimming and eventually waterbreathing. for thousands of years, they are evolved to what now people known as The Merfolks

They still live in their ancestral homes, it still has bridges they uses, but they swam now, they don't use them anymore
