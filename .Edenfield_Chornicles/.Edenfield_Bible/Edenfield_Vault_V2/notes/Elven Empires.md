the Elven Tribes moved from their Illusory Forests once their plains are flooded, those who moved either goes to the Central or Western side of Archaea. those who chose to remain are evolved to what is known to be Merfolks. Merfolks are the closest remaining relatives to the Proto-Elves

The Merfolks live to the submerged Illusory Forests, where they survives with magic alone, they have little to no technology, and have very minimal contacts to the outside world
