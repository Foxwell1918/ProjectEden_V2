The Twist in Edenfield is that "The Boy Who lived" is not the main character, but his sidekick, that is Will. Will is a Prince, one of the Twin Princes, and is considered to be a piece of a prophecy.

Will hates the Solivian tradition of hierarchy and feodalism, and so he got himself a Scholarship to Argantheia to escape and learn in a place that is more relaxed and don't know who he was.

Willard de Avon and Konrad de Avon. is the name of Solivian Twin Princes. with Willard being the older twin, therefore the heir to the throne, in which Will is not going to take.
