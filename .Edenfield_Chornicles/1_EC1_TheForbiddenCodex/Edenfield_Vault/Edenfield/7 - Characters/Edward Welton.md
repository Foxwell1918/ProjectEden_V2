## Character Reference

### **Frodo Baggins** 
- An underdog, reluctant Volunteer. There are more than one Prince with ancient Glyndorian lineage. "Something Elvish" His virtue is not in his birthright. But his deeds.

	**Courageous (Quiet Heroism):** Unlike warriors such as Aragorn or Gimli, Frodo’s bravery is moral and spiritual—enduring suffering for the greater good.
	
	**Humble & Self-Sacrificing:** Frodo never seeks glory; he accepts his burden reluctantly but with resolve.
	
    **Resilient & Enduring:** Though not strong physically, he possesses immense inner strength and persistence.

- ### **Ikal** 
	**Dreamer & Idealist:** Eddie is deeply imaginative, often thinking beyond the constraints of poverty and his environment. 
	
	**Loyal & Compassionate:** Cares deeply for his friends in _Laskar Pelangi_ and often empathizes with their struggles.
	
	**Resilient & Determined:** Despite systemic obstacles (poverty, inequality, corruption), Ikal never gives up on pursuing knowledge and self-fulfillment.

- ### **Harry Potter**
	- **Brave & Impulsive:** Harry often leaps into danger to protect others, sometimes without fully thinking things through.
	
	- **Independent but Stubborn:** Struggles to accept help, often feeling he must carry burdens alone.
	
	- **Resilient & Determined:** Despite trauma and loss, he persists in fighting for what’s right.
