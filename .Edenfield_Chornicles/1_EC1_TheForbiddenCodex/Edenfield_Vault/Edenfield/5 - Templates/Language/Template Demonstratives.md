## Usage

- **As Adjectives**: Placed before the noun (e.g., *this book*, *those houses*).  
- **As Pronouns**: Stand alone to replace the noun (e.g., *this is mine*).  
- **In Emphasis/Contrast**: Can carry emphasis when combined with particles or stress.
