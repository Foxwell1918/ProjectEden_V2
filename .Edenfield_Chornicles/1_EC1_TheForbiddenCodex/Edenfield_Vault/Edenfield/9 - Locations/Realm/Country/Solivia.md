## Country Reference

### Gondor
The capital city, Minas Tirith, embodies Gondor’s grandeur with its seven-tiered construction and White Tower. Osgiliath, once the capital, fell into ruin following wars and plague. Other important cities include Pelargir, Dol Amroth, and Minas Ithil (later Minas Morgul).

Gondorian culture values order, discipline, and tradition, with Minas Tirith as its capital. The city is a symbol of strength and endurance, built in seven levels of white stone and crowned by the Citadel. Gondor’s people are skilled in warfare, particularly in defending their borders against Mordor, Harad, and other hostile lands. Knights, Rangers, and soldiers form the backbone of society, though Gondor also preserves rich traditions in law, lore, and architecture.
