## Country Reference

## Rohirrim
The Rohirrim are a hardy, warlike people who live in a pastoral, agrarian society. Their livelihood depends largely on horse-breeding, cattle-herding, and farming in the plains of Rohan.

Their culture is oral, with songs, poetry, and legends playing a central role in preserving history. The Rohirrim are described as tall, fair-haired, and often spoke in a language related to that of the ancient Northmen; Tolkien represented their tongue through Old English.

Hospitality, honor, and loyalty to kin and king are core values. Their dwellings are typically wooden halls, the greatest of which is **Meduseld**, the Golden Hall of Edoras.
