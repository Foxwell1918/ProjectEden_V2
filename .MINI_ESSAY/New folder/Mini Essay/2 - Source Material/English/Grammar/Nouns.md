# Nouns

A noun is anything with a Name. 
Nouns are People or living things, places, things, or Ideas.

ex:
This is Raul
He is from Argentina
He is a penguin.
Raul has big dreams

Nouns: Raul, Argentina, Penguin, Dreams

## Singular or Plural Nouns

Singular meaning there are only one
Plural came from the root word "Plus" meaning "More" which means there are more than one.
