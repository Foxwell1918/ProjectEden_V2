Being Articulate means being able to convey an Idea or a feeling fluently and coherently. Fluently, meaning that you are articulating your Ideas with ease and grace in your delivery. 

**Let's start with Coherency**
Coherently meaning that the message, structure and content of your articulation remains logical and easy to follow. 

There are a few steps in achieving this
1. Understanding the thing you want to convey.
   If you won't be able to convey something clearly, if you didn't understand it in the first place. Good Communications Begins With Good Understanding.
