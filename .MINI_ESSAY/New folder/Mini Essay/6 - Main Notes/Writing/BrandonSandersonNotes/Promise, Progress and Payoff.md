# Promise, Progress and Payoff


- **Promise**: The *initial expectation* set by the story, often through the opening scene, tone, or early chapters.
- **Progress**: The *movement* toward fulfilling that promise, measured by character actions, obstacles overcome, or information revealed.
- **Payoff**: The *resolution* that fulfills the promise, ideally surprising yet inevitable, satisfying the reader’s expectations.

Sanderson stresses that **these three elements** are **interdependent**. A failure in one often results in a story feeling unsatisfying or disjointed.

This is similar to Will's Three Pillars of Story, where Will's version is
- Character
- Obstacle
- Goal
