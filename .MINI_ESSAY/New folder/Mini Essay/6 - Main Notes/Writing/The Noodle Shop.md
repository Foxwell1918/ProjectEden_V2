# The Noodle Shop

There i was, sitting in front of the noodle shop by the side of the road. My acting instructor had told me to be more mindful, and so i tried. It was two days ago, i went there for a lunch.

The shop itself was pretty small, there is a dorm at the back, possibly for workers or students. The owners had known me since i was a regular. I usually orders chickens, except that day. That day i ordered a simple instant noodle considering i was craving one.

The noodle came with an omlette and the seasonings at the top, which is a bit different than the other Noodle Shop. who usually put them at the bottom.

The noodle is soft and cooked pretty well, and this particular brand of instant noodle has a more kick into it, and their crunch is far better. Added to that is the omlette they make, they put more work into the noodle than any other noodle shop around.
