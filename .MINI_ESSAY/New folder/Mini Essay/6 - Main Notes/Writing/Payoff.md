# Payoff

Payoffs are just giving people a surprising yet inevitable fulfillment of your promises.

You make it surprising with lots of ways, one of em is the Twist

One of them is giving the Plausable Deniability
Make them believe that it won't happen.

Escalations. Obstacles. Red Herrings.
