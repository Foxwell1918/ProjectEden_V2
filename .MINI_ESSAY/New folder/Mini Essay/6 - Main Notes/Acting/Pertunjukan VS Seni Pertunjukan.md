# Pertunjukan VS Seni Pertunjukan

### "Pertunjukan"

From the name itself, it came from the root word "Tunjuk" meaning to point.
Therefore, in a "Pertunjukan" or show, there is someone who points at something. and something that is being pointed at.

so therefore, in a show, there are 3 elements:
1. The Pointer
2. The Thing being Pointed at
3. The setting.

"Pertunjukan" in it on itself, is an act where an individual or a thing getting an attention. Me talking to my teacher is him giving me a show of him teaching.

### "Seni Pertunjukan"

Let's break down the term Seni Pertunjukan. We have discussed what does "Pertunjukan" means according to my mentor Alec.

What is the difference between "Pertunjukan" and "Seni Pertunjukan"? It is in the added term "Seni" or "Arts"

Now we can go to a javanese term called "Karawitan" with the root word being "Rawit" meaning small, or implying smallness.

So therefore. Art is something large made small for human consumption.

If "Pertunjukan" means everyday life of human interaction. "Seni Pertunjukan" means an everyday life of human interaction that is captured in a small consumeable way.

Art is a snapshot of everyday life that is captured in a small way.

You cannot write a book telling of somone's life from birth to death, although you can and it's called an "Anthology" but, you cannot include every event that happens in his life. You can only select a few ones that supports the theme of the Anthology. Therefore capturing the life of someone, in a book.

A picture is limited by the smallness of the frame.

A film is limited by the smallness of its runtime.

A game is limited by the smallenss of its tech.

A fiction is limited by the imagination of its consumer.

But all of them are Arts.
