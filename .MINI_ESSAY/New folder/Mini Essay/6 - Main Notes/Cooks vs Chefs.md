# Cooks vs Chefs

In one of Brandon Sanderson's philosophy is to train a Writer to be a Chef and not a cook, why?

Cooks only follows recipes. A great cook can make a really delicious hamburgers down to the portions. But a great Hamburger Cook can only make hamburgers, if one day a costumer came in and requested a steak, he'd be confused on how to make one, and is more likely to make a subpar steak.

Whereas a Chef knows the fundamental of cooking, the basics of taste, textures and culture. And therefore can adapts to cook so many different cuisines and food because he knows the settings of each cooking, how each food tastes, what's the textures like, how can one make it more appealing. If a Chef makes steak for a living in his restaurant, and one day a customer came in and requests a hamburger, he knows how to make a damn good hamburger.

In Writing, becoming a Chef means understanding the tools of literary works, experimenting with them.
