## Poetry is a Special Mode of Literature.

It is a distinct mode of literature that operates differently from prose. Whilst prose such as novels, essays, articles etc relies on straightforward narrative structures.

Poetry emplys [[Verses]] Characterized by specific patterns, rhythms, and forms. This mode demands different kind of reading, one that recognizes its formal constraints and artistic nounances.

## The Shape of Language.

The [[Shape of Language]] is a defining feature of Poetry.
Is that sound and sense areinseparatable.
Unlike prose, where meaning can often be understood independently of sound, poetry's aural qualities are integral to its impact and meaning

Throughout history, critics and poets have offered various definitions, emphasizing different aspects of poetry:

| **Critic/Poet**    | **Main Idea**                                                                                        | **Quote / Explanation**                                             |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Giovanni Boccaccio | Poetry is **exquisitely wrought** and **composed under a veil**.                                     | *Poetry is something carefully crafted, with artistry and finesse.* |
| Sir Philip Sidney  | Poetry is **a speaking picture**—visual, imagistic, sensorial.                                       | *It creates vivid images through words.*                            |
| Samuel Johnson     | Literature **teaches the art of living**; poetry shares this humane, practical wisdom.               | *Poetry connects us to human life and experience.*                  |
| Wordsworth         | Poetry is **the spontaneous overflow of powerful feelings** recollected in tranquility.              | *It arises naturally from emotion, not premeditated.*               |
| Coleridge          | Poetry is **the best words in the best order**—highlighting the importance of **artistry**.          | *Form and content are inseparable; craftsmanship matters.*          |
| Percy Shelley      | Poetry **redeems from decay** the divine in man, acting as a mediator between humans and the divine. | *It has a transcendent, spiritual dimension.*                       |
| Matthew Arnold     | Poetry is **the criticism of life**—an appreciation, not critique, of human existence.               | *It reflects and elevates our understanding of life.*               |

Despite the differing views, there is an agreement that

Poetry is a distinct mode of communication, requiring special attention and engagement beyond ordinary language.
