# Writing the Poem

Poetry starts when you have something in your mind that you want to sit down with and write. 
Carry a little notebook and something to write with.
You're not a poet all the time, but you can be a poet when you're not writing poetry.
You might find an image, or something to write it.

## Jump startinfg your writing:

How to start writing poetry
You can open yourself up and settle your mind down like meditatively, it sometimes don't work
Thre's a more active way, to trigger your writing
one is to take a poem you like, and then just write an immitation of it
Or use the first line of it
Or write a five first lines which will lead to second lines.
At the end of the day or morning, just take a piece of paper and write 20 things you did the previous day.
The main thing is not to go Chronologically
Goes to the orders of things that it comes up in your mind
If oyour read it, there might be something that trigger something for a material
But in any case, you are actively writing.

## Start at the beginning.

Billy's practice is to use the Queen of Heart talking to Alice
"Start at the beginning, and when you come to the end, Stop."
That is pretty muhc how Billy write.
The beginning lines lead to another and another.

The reason Billy write in one sitting is that if the Poem is a continous act that akes place in a certain time, that the reader will sense it, that the poem is the product of someone thinking it out and not working it out.

## Let the Poem Guide you

Remember that when you write poem, yhere's no such thng as a distraction
If something distracts you, put it in the poem.
Loose grip on the pen.
Youre able to take hint from the poem so it can leads you to another
Poems written by imagination moves to the direction that is unpredictable.

No surprise for the Writer, no surprise for the Reader.

Hold your pen lightly.
