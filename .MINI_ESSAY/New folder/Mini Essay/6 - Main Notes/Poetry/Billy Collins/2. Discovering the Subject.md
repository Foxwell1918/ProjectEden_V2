## Write What you want to write

If you're thinking too much on what is allowed in Poetry, you shouldn't. 
You don't wanna thin too much about it
You don't want to adhere the rules so much that you're afraid to write.
Don't think too much on what should be poetry
Let poetry do what you want to say.
You can do anything in Poetry.

## The Subject you start with, And the Subject you Discover.

The progress of hte poem is often from the small thing to the large thing
From a material thing like a cup or a kettle
Into a Bigger thing, like fear, abandonment, dread.

A poem has two subject.
1. The subject you start with
	 Just a provisional to begin the poem, quickly abandoned once that beginning leads to what the poem is all about.
	 .
	 If you want to make a poem about human suffering, don't start it with human suffering. Begin with what you see out of the window, before beginning into it.
	 Often one thing happens happens before another, it often leads you into something deeper.
	 
2. The subject you Discover
	 This is the deep shit that you're going to talk about in your poetry, the 2nd to 3rd plot of your poetry, where the theme starts to hit.

## Don't think about Theme

At some point, when ones composing a poetry, one dont' usually think about Themes.
How does a poem Go, how does a Poem behaves.
How a poem develops.

## Know when to be Clear, and When to be Mysterious.

Clarity and Mystery both has a place in poetry. 
The poet knows the difference between the Clear and Mysterious
What cards to turn over, what cards to leave face-down.
