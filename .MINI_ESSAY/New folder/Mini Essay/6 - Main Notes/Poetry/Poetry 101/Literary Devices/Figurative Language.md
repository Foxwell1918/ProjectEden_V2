# Figurative Language

Figurative Language is also persuasive. You can use Figurative Language as a Persuasive Language

What is Figurative Language?

Figure of speech. 

What is Figure?

A figure is something that has a shape

so Figure in Figurative language means a "Shape of a Language"

There are various shape and sizes in a language that we can shape and mold within a language with the purpose of communicating something.

We can get various shape in a Language.

We can stretch and we can mold Language. Language is Elastic, Flexible. To fit it into something that we want to communicate.

The purpose of Figurative Language is to communicate some idea in a Creative and Evocative way. It's More creative, so it is more vivid. It evokes an Image or an Idea. Because it is memorable. We see the Shape of a Word.

ex: Metaphor.

Metaphor is comparing two things using Is or To be verbs, or assuming a To-be verbs. Sometimes they don't say the word exactly. but from the structure of the sentence, there are two things being compared.

A is transformed into B
![[Pasted image 20250908163517.png]]

Usually these two things are an Ideas, with Object, things or person. 

the idea being discussed is "My Love" that is being transformed into "A red, red rose."

How do you understand the Point of a Metaphor?

You need to pay attention to the Thing that it becomes. You need to concentrate to the Concrete, Physical details of the object.

All Metaphors are Arguments.

the word IS is the arguments.

Roses are red, thorny and pretty. Picking up a rose will hurt your fingers, such as loving someone will hurt.
