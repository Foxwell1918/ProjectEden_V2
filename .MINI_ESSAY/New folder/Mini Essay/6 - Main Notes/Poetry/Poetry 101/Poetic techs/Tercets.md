# Tercets

Tercets is a stanza that is composed of Three Lines.

an English-Language heiku is an example of unrhymed tercet poem.

Triplets is a Tercet that has three lines with the same rhyme. AAA. Triplets are a bit rare.

Other types of Tercets includes an enclosed tercets with the rhyme ABA pattern. Where the verse is continued in the next by making the outer lines ofd the next stanza rhyme with the central line making BCB.
