# Rhythm

Rhythm in poetry is the recurrence of Specific sounds based on long and short patterns of stressed and unstressed syllables. Specifically in poetry in verse form.

Beats provide the rhythm in poetry as Rhythm is determined by the ear, and has to do with the poem's tempo and structure.
