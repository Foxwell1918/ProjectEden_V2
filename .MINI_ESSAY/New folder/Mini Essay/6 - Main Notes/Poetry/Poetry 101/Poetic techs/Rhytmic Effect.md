# Rhytmic Effect

A rhytmic effect is the influence that the timing and duration of events have on perception, attention and cognitive processing, particularily in music and language.

These effects are explained by the Dynamic Attending Theory.

Which suggest that internal neural rhythms can synchronize with external rhythmic stimuli, causing attention to peak at predictable moments.
