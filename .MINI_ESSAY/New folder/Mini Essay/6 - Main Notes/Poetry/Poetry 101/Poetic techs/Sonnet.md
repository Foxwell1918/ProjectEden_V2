# Sonnet

A sonnet is a fixed poetic form with a structure traditionally consisting of Fourteen Lines adhereing to a set of [[Rhyming Scheme]].

The term derives from the Italian word _sonetto_ (lit. 'little song', from the Latin word _sonus_, lit. 'sound')
