# Iambs

Iambs are one unstressed syllable followed by one stressed syllable. Most common in English poetry
	 *da-DA*

It also can be referred to a Short Syllable followed by a Long Syllable.

ex:

the word "Attempt" is a natural Iamb

At - Tempt where At is unstressed and Tempt is Stressed
