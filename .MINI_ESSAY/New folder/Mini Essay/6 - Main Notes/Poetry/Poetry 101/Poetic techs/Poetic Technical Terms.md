# Poetic Technical Terms

Poems are divided into [[Verses]] kind of like music.

[[Stanza]] is a group of lines in a poem, used to organize the poem's ideas and create structure. And depending on how the stanzas are divided,  there are different terminologies:

1. [[Couplet]].
	A couplet is a two-line stanza that often rhymes (typically end rhymes) but not usually have to.
2. [[Tercets]].
	 Tercets  are a three-line stanza.
3. [[Quatrain]].
	 A Quatrain is a four-line stanza.
4. [[Sonnet]].
	 A Sonnet is a 14-line stanza that can be a single poem. 

While verses and stanzas subdivides into lines.
Lines subdivides into [[Metrical Foot]] or Feet.

1. Verses
2. Stanzas
3. Foot

**Feets** are the basic unit of measurment in Poetry. basically the count of the poetry. The Beat of Poetry.

Feets are usually follows patterns of Stresses and Emphasis. and there are different kinds, mostly are:

1. **[[Iambs]]**
	 One unstressed syllable followed by one stressed syllable. Most common in English poetry
	 *da-DA*
2. **Trochee**
	 The reverse of an Iamb. A stressed syllable followed by an unstressed syllable.
	 *DA-da*
3. **Anapest**
	 Two unstressed syllables followed by a stressed syllable.
	 *da-da-DA*
4. **Dactyl**
	 The reverse of an Anapest. One stressed syllable followed by two unstressed syllables.
	 *DA-da-da*
5. **Spondee**
	 Two stressed syllable.
	 *DA-DA*

These are the kinds of feets that we can have in poetry.

The pattern of poetic stressed is called a "[[Meter]]" and much like music, the sound patterns that restults are called "Rhythm" 

Meter is common in english poetry. here is an illustration on how to think about feet.
**Iambic Pentameter** includes fiver (Penta) Iambs (Iambic), meaning we have five sets of unstressed syllables followed by stressed syllables. Shakespeare mostly use this.

ex: *"If MUsic BE the FOOD of LOVE, play ON"*

That is an Iambic Pentameter that doesn't rhyme.

A poem that doesn't Rhyme is called a "Blank Verse."

Some poems have a Rhyme Scheme, some don't. 

When you look at the elements of the lines, thythm, meter, and evertyhing discussed. It's called Scansion.
